# 🏨 Gujarat Hotel Analytics Dashboard – Power BI Project

Welcome to my latest Power BI project focused on the **Hospitality Industry**.

This dashboard analyzes **1,930 hotels across Gujarat**, uncovering insights related to hotel pricing, guest ratings, customer reviews, facilities, and hotel distribution. By transforming raw hotel data into meaningful visualizations, the project provides a clear view of Gujarat's hospitality landscape and highlights key tourism and accommodation trends.

---

# 🎯 Project Objective

- 🏨 Analyze hotel distribution across major cities and tourist destinations.
- 💰 Identify premium and budget-friendly hotel markets.
- ⭐ Evaluate customer satisfaction using ratings and reviews.
- 🛎️ Discover the most popular facilities offered by hotels.
- 📊 Transform hotel data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards.

# 📊 Key Insights

### 🏨 Hotel Distribution Insights

- 🏆 Ahmedabad leads Gujarat with **480 hotels**.
- 🥈 Surat follows with **282 hotels**.
- 🥉 Gandhinagar ranks third with **261 hotels**.
- 🌆 Ahmedabad, Surat, and Gandhinagar together host **1,023 hotels**, representing **53% of Gujarat's total hotels**.

### 💰 Pricing Insights

- 💎 Saputara has the highest average hotel price at **₹6,822**.
- 💎 Navsari follows closely with an average price of **₹6,605**.
- 📈 Tourist destinations generally command higher hotel prices than business-focused locations.
- 💰 Saputara and Navsari emerge as Gujarat's premium hotel markets.

### ⭐ Rating Insights

- ⭐ Hotels maintain an average rating of **3.54** across Gujarat.
- 👍 **1,324 hotels** fall under the **Very Good** category.
- 📊 Nearly **69% of hotels** are rated "Very Good," reflecting positive guest experiences.
- 🏆 Pardi, Kamrej achieved the highest average rating of **5.0**.

### 📝 Review Insights

- 📝 Hotels received a total of **2,365 customer reviews**.
- 🌟 Hotels in the "Very Good" category account for the majority of customer feedback.
- 📈 Higher-rated destinations generally show stronger customer engagement.

### 🛎️ Facility Insights

| Facility | Hotels Offering |
|-----------|-----------|
| 🛎️ 24-Hour Room Service | 75 |
| 🍽️ Restaurant | 47 |
| 🏋️ Gym | 15 |
| 🏊 Shared Swimming Pool | 11 |
| 💆 Spa | 11 |

- 📌 24-Hour Room Service is the most commonly available facility.
- 🍽️ Restaurants are the second most popular facility offered by hotels.
