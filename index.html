🏨 Gujarat Hotel Analytics Dashboard

This project analyzes 1,930 hotels across Gujarat using an interactive Power BI dashboard to uncover insights related to hotel pricing, guest ratings, customer reviews, facilities, and hospitality trends. The dashboard helps identify premium hotel markets, top-rated destinations, popular facilities, and customer satisfaction patterns, enabling data-driven decision-making in the hospitality industry.

📊 Key Highlights

🏨 1,930 Hotels Analyzed
💰 Average Hotel Price: ₹3.44K
⭐ Average Rating: 3.54
📝 Total Reviews: 2,365
🌆 Ahmedabad, Surat & Gandhinagar account for 53% of Gujarat's hotels
💎 Saputara has the highest average hotel price (₹6,822)
🏆 Pardi, Kamrej achieved the highest average rating (5.0)
