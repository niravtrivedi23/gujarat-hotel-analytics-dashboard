🏨 Gujarat Hotel Analytics Dashboard

This project analyzes 1,930 hotels across Gujarat using an interactive Power BI Dashboard.

The analysis focuses on hotel pricing, guest ratings, customer reviews, facilities, hotel distribution, and hospitality trends to uncover meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps identify premium hotel markets, top-rated destinations, popular facilities, and customer satisfaction patterns that support data-driven decision-making in the hospitality industry.

📊 Key Highlights

🏨 Total Hotels: 1,930
💰 Average Hotel Price: ₹3.44K
⭐ Average Rating: 3.54
📝 Total Reviews: 2,365
🌆 Ahmedabad, Surat & Gandhinagar account for 53% of Gujarat's hotels
💎 Saputara has the highest average hotel price (₹6,822)
🏆 Pardi, Kamrej achieved the highest average rating (5.0)
